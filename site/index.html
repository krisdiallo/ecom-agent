Why it happens

A model given a thin brief fills the gaps with an average of everything it has read about your category. Average copy is the default output, not a failure state. The fix isn't a cleverer prompt — it's closing the three gaps that produce generic writing: who specifically buys, what they're comparing you to, and what you can actually prove.
