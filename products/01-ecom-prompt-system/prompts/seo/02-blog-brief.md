# Content Brief for a Ranking Post
**Use when:** commissioning or writing a post that must both rank and sell.

```
[STORE BRIEF]
TARGET QUERY + what the searcher actually wants (task, not keyword):
TOP 3 RANKING RESULTS (paste headings — you need to see the bar):
WHICH PRODUCT this post should lead toward, and honestly, how naturally it does:

Produce:
1. Search intent in one sentence. If the intent is informational and the product fit is
   forced, say so — a post that ranks and sells nothing is fine; a post that sells nothing
   AND ranks nothing because it pivots to a pitch in paragraph two is not.
2. Outline with H2/H3s, each with the specific question it answers.
3. What the top results all miss — this is the only reason your post deserves to outrank them.
4. Where the product mention belongs, and the sentence that earns it.
5. 5 internal links; 3 external sources worth citing.
6. Word count justified by the query, not by a rule of thumb.

RULE: no "ultimate guide" framing unless the outline earns it.
```
