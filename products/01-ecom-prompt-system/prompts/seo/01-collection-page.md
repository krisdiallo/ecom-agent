# Collection / Category Page Copy

**Use when:** category pages are thin and don't rank. **Output:** intro, buying guide, FAQ, links.

---

## Prompt

```
Write copy for an ecommerce collection page that must serve two readers at once: a shopper
scanning for the right product, and a search engine deciding whether this page deserves to
rank. The shopper wins any conflict.

[STORE BRIEF]

COLLECTION:
- Name and URL:
- Products in it (names + key differences between them):
- Primary search term and 3 related terms:
- What makes choosing hard for a buyer in this category:

OUTPUT:
1. INTRO (60–90 words, above the grid). Must help someone choose, not describe the category.
2. BUYING GUIDE (below the grid, 250–400 words): the 3–4 decisions a buyer must make,
   and how to make each. Reference actual products by name where one is the clear answer.
3. FAQ: 5 questions people genuinely ask before buying in this category. Real answers, not
   restated marketing. Mark any answer that needs a fact I haven't given you as [NEED].
4. INTERNAL LINKS: 5 anchor-text suggestions pointing to other collections/products,
   phrased as things a reader would actually click.

RULES:
- No keyword stuffing. The primary term appears naturally or not at all.
- Do not open with "When it comes to..." or "In the world of...".
- Do not claim a product is best-selling, award-winning, or highly rated unless I said so.
- Write for someone who will read 40 words and then scroll. Front-load.

SELF-CHECK: which sentences exist only for SEO and give the shopper nothing? List and cut.
```
