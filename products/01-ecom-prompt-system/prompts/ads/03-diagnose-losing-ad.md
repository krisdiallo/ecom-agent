# Diagnose a Losing Ad
**Use when:** spend is going out and nothing is coming back. Diagnosis before rewriting.

```
[STORE BRIEF]
THE AD (creative description + full copy):
METRICS: impressions, CTR, CPC, add-to-cart rate, CPA, ROAS, spend, days running:
BENCHMARKS from your other ads:
LANDING PAGE the ad points to (paste the top of it):

Diagnose in this order and STOP at the first stage that's broken — fixing a later stage
while an earlier one leaks is how budgets disappear:
1. Delivery: is it getting impressions at all? If not, it's a bidding/audience issue, not creative.
2. Attention (CTR vs benchmark): the hook or the visual is failing.
3. Interest (landing page bounce): the ad wrote a cheque the page didn't cash. Name the
   specific promise the page fails to repeat.
4. Intent (add-to-cart): offer, price, or trust problem — not an ad problem.
5. Purchase (checkout drop): shipping cost, payment options, or account-required friction.

Output: the broken stage, the evidence, the ONE change to make, and what metric should
move if you're right. Then: "If this doesn't move in N days, the real problem is ___."

RULE: If spend is too low for the numbers to mean anything, say so and state the volume
needed. Reading noise as signal is more expensive than waiting.
```
