# Supplier & Vendor Emails
**Use when:** negotiating MOQs, chasing a late shipment, or raising a quality issue.

```
CONTEXT: relationship length, order volume, payment terms, your leverage (honestly assessed):
SITUATION:
WHAT YOU WANT (the actual outcome, not the opening ask):
WHAT YOU'D ACCEPT:

Write the email:
- Subject naming the order/PO and the decision needed.
- First sentence states the ask. No relationship throat-clearing before the point.
- Facts with dates and numbers. Attach-worthy specifics, not adjectives.
- A dated deadline and what happens after it.
- Tone: direct, not aggressive. You want the next order to go well too.

Then give me: the likely counter-offer, and my response to it.

RULE: assess my leverage honestly. If I'm a small account with no alternative supplier
lined up, an ultimatum is a bluff they will call, and you should tell me that instead of
writing me a confident email that loses the relationship.
```
