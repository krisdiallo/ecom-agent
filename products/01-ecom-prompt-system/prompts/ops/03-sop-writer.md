# SOP Writer
**Use when:** you're the bottleneck and need to hand a task off.

```
TASK TO DOCUMENT:
WALK ME THROUGH how you currently do it, including the parts you do by instinct:
TOOLS + access required:
WHAT GOES WRONG when it's done badly:

Produce an SOP a competent new hire can follow without asking you a question:
1. Trigger — what starts this, and how they know.
2. Numbered steps. Every step is one action with an observable result.
3. Decision points as explicit if/then rules. Your instinct is the expensive part to
   transfer — interrogate me until every "it depends" becomes a rule.
4. Definition of done.
5. Escalation: the three situations where they should stop and ask you.
6. The two most likely mistakes and how to catch them before they matter.

RULE: where I described something vaguely, ask me a direct question rather than writing a
plausible step. A confident wrong SOP is worse than no SOP — it gets followed.
```
