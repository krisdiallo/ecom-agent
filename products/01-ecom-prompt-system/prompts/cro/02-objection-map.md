# Objection Map
**Use when:** you need to know what to say, before deciding where to say it.

```
[STORE BRIEF]
PRICE and the cheaper alternative buyers consider:
SUPPORT TICKETS / pre-purchase questions you actually receive (paste raw):
REVIEWS mentioning hesitation (paste, including the critical ones):

Build a table:
| Objection | Buyer's actual words | % of buyers likely blocked | Best answer | Where it belongs |

Rank by buyers blocked. Then:
- Which objections are answered NOWHERE on the site right now?
- Which are answered but too far down the page to matter?
- Which cannot be answered with copy and require a policy change (free returns, warranty,
  a size chart, a cheaper tier)? Name those separately — a policy problem disguised as a
  copy problem eats months.

RULE: use the buyer's language verbatim from the tickets and reviews. Your paraphrase is
always more flattering than what they said, and less persuasive for exactly that reason.
```
