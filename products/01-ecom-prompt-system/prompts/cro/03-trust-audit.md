# Trust Signal Audit
**Use when:** the store is new, or traffic is cold and bounces.

```
[STORE BRIEF]
PASTE: header, footer, product page, cart, checkout — all visible text.
LIST: badges, guarantees, policies, contact methods, social proof currently shown.

Assess as a first-time visitor who has never heard of this brand and has been scammed before:
1. Within 5 seconds, what evidence exists that a real business is behind this?
2. Before entering a card, what's still unresolved? (returns, contact, delivery, who they are)
3. Which trust signals are decorative rather than load-bearing? (unverifiable badges,
   stock photos, "as seen in" with no link, review counts with no reviews)
4. What real trust asset do you have that isn't shown anywhere?

Output the 5 highest-impact additions, each with exact placement and exact wording.

RULE: do not suggest fabricating social proof, badges, or urgency. Fake trust signals are
detected by the exact skeptical buyers you're trying to convert, and the detection is
permanent. If the store genuinely lacks proof, the recommendation is how to earn some.
```
