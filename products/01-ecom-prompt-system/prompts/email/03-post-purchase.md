# Post-Purchase Flow
**Use when:** you have buyers but no second orders. Cheapest revenue in the store.

```
[STORE BRIEF]
PRODUCT PURCHASED + what a first-time buyer usually gets wrong with it:
SHIPPING TIMELINE:
NATURAL REPURCHASE / COMPLEMENT WINDOW:
REVIEW REQUEST TOOL:

Write:
1. Confirmation — set the delivery expectation precisely. Vague shipping language generates
   more support tickets than any other email in ecommerce.
2. Shipped — tracking plus one thing to know before it arrives.
3. Delivered +2 days — how to get the best result from it. No selling. This email exists to
   prevent the return.
4. +10 days — review request. Ask for one specific thing ("what nearly stopped you buying")
   rather than "leave a review." Specific asks get answered.
5. At the repurchase window — the complement or refill, framed by what they already own.

RULES: no upsell before delivery. Never ask for a review before they've used it — you'll
harvest the frustration of someone who hasn't figured it out yet.
```
