# Welcome Sequence (4 emails)
**Use when:** new subscribers get a discount code and then silence.

```
[STORE BRIEF]
SIGNUP SOURCE (popup / checkout / lead magnet — this changes intent completely):
INCENTIVE OFFERED, if any:
YOUR ORIGIN STORY, in facts not adjectives:
BEST-SELLING PRODUCTS + why each sells:

Write 4 emails:
1. Immediate — deliver what was promised in the first 20 words. Everything else is secondary.
2. Day 2 — the story, but only the parts that explain why the product is better. Founder
   narrative that doesn't resolve into a product benefit is self-indulgent and gets unsubscribes.
3. Day 4 — proof. Real reviews and real numbers from the brief only.
4. Day 7 — the offer, with the reason it expires. If there's no honest reason, don't invent one;
   use a genuine constraint (season, stock, batch) or drop the deadline entirely.

Per email: 3 subject lines (<45 chars), preview text that adds information, body, one CTA.
RULES: One idea per email. No "just checking in." Assume mobile, dark mode, 8-second scan.
SELF-CHECK: unsupported claims, and any email that could be deleted without breaking the arc.
```
