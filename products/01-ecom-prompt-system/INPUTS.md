# Your Store Brief

Fill this in once. Paste it into any prompt where it says `[STORE BRIEF]`.
Ten minutes here saves you every generic output you'd otherwise have to rewrite.

---

**Store:** <name, URL>
**What you sell:** <one sentence a stranger would understand>
**Price band:** <e.g. $28–$95, AOV ~$61>

**Who buys (be specific — "women 25–45" is not specific):**
- Trigger that makes them shop: <what just happened in their life>
- What they're comparing you to: <named competitors AND the do-nothing option>
- What they're afraid of: <sizing, quality, shipping time, being scammed>

**Proof you actually have:**
- Reviews: <count, avg rating, 2 verbatim quotes>
- Numbers you can legally claim: <units sold, years in business, return rate>
- Certifications/materials/origin: <only what's documented>

**Voice:**
- Words you use: <...>
- Words you'd never use: <...>
- Closest brand voice reference: <...>

**Hard constraints:**
- Claims you legally cannot make: <health, sustainability, "best", comparisons>
- Compliance: <FTC endorsements, allergen, region-specific>

---

## Why the brief matters
Every bad AI output for ecommerce comes from the model filling a context gap with an
average of the internet. The brief closes the gaps that matter: who, versus what, and
what you can prove. Prompts in this pack assume it exists.
