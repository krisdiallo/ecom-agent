## Description

You already tried using AI for your product pages. You got copy that said your product
would "elevate your everyday routine," invented a material you don't use, and could have
been describing anyone's product.

That's not a model problem. It's a context problem — and prompts that don't fix it will
keep producing the same average-of-the-internet output no matter which AI you use.

**What this is:** 17 prompts and 4 workflows built for people who run a store. Each prompt
takes a structured brief, refuses to invent facts, and ends with a self-check that catches
the specific ways AI output fails in ecommerce.

**The self-check is the part you're actually buying.** Every prompt ends by asking the model
to list the claims it made without evidence, and to flag any sentence that would still make
sense with a competitor's name in it. Those sentences are why AI copy sounds like AI copy.
