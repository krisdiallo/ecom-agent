# Playbook — It's not a conversion problem
Triggered when Surface 1 scores under 40 and Surface 2 scores over 70.

## What's happening
Your pages work. You're sending people who were never going to buy. This is the most common
misdiagnosis in ecommerce, and it's expensive precisely because the "fix" — rewriting pages —
feels productive and generates visible work for weeks.

## How to confirm before acting
- Segment conversion rate **by source**. If one source converts at 2% and another at 0.1%,
  you don't have a page problem, you have a source problem.
- Check the top queries or ad terms. Are they buying-intent or research-intent?
- Check the mismatch: does the ad promise something the page doesn't repeat in its first screen?

## Fixes
1. **Cut the worst source.** Not optimize — cut. Reallocate to the source already converting.
   The single highest-ROI action in this entire kit, and it takes ten minutes.
2. **Fix the promise mismatch.** If the ad says "waterproof commuter bag" and the page opens
   with brand story, the page is fine and the sequence is broken.
3. **Build a page per intent** rather than sending everything to one page.
4. **Only then** return to the product page audit.

## The honest version
If your total sessions are under a few hundred a week, no audit in this kit can tell you much —
the numbers are noise. Your problem is demand, and the work is distribution, not optimization.
I'd rather say that than sell you a fourth week of page tweaks.
