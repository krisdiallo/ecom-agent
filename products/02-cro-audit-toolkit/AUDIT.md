# The Audit — 62 checks, 7 surfaces

Score each 0/1/2 (see `SCORING.md`). ★ = weighted ×3. Do the ★ checks alone for the 20-minute
fast path. Open your store on a phone, not a desktop, and go in order.

---
## Surface 1 — Traffic Quality (7 checks)
*Audit this first. If it fails, everything downstream is measuring the wrong people.*

1. ★ Do your top 3 traffic sources match your top 3 revenue sources? A source sending 40% of
   sessions and 4% of revenue is the problem, not the page it lands on.
2. ★ Does the landing page repeat the specific promise the ad or listing made? Name the promise.
3. Is branded vs non-branded search separated in reporting? Branded traffic flatters every metric.
4. Do paid campaigns exclude existing customers, or are you buying people who'd have returned free?
5. Is there a landing page per major intent, or does everything dump onto the homepage?
6. Are you measuring conversion by source, or only sitewide? Sitewide CR is an average that hides both.
7. Do your top 10 organic queries describe a buyer, or a browser? "How to clean X" is not a buyer.

## Surface 2 — Product Page (13 checks)
8. ★ Within 5 seconds on mobile: is it clear what it is, what it costs, and why it's worth it?
9. ★ Is total delivered cost visible before checkout, including shipping?
10. ★ Are the 3 questions that most block purchase answered above the fold or one scroll down?
11. Do images show scale, use, and the thing a buyer is worried about (fit, texture, size)?
12. Is there an image showing the product in the buyer's actual context, not on white?
13. Are specs complete enough that nobody must email you to buy?
14. Is variant selection unambiguous — can a buyer tell what they're choosing without guessing?
15. Are reviews on the page, not behind a tab nobody clicks?
16. Are critical reviews visible? All-5-star review sets reduce trust; buyers read the 3-stars.
17. Is there a "who this is not for" statement? Pre-filtering raises conversion and cuts returns.
18. Does the copy survive the swap test — would it still be true with a competitor's name in it?
19. Is the return policy stated on the product page, not only in the footer?
20. Is the add-to-cart button visible without hunting on a mid-size phone?

## Surface 3 — Cart (8 checks)
21. ★ Is shipping cost shown or calculable in the cart, before checkout starts?
22. ★ Is the free-shipping threshold shown with the gap to it ("$12 away")?
23. Can quantity and variants be edited without leaving the cart?
24. Is there a visible path back to shopping that doesn't lose the cart?
25. Are carts persistent across sessions and devices?
26. Is there exactly one primary action? Competing CTAs in a cart cost conversions.
27. Are upsells relevant and quiet, or do they interrupt a decision already made?
28. Are trust signals (returns, secure payment, delivery window) present at the cart step?

## Surface 4 — Checkout (9 checks)
29. ★ Is guest checkout available without creating an account?
30. ★ Are there any surprise costs at the final step? This is the single biggest abandonment cause.
31. ★ Is a delivery date or window given, not just a shipping method name?
32. Is the form asking only for fields you genuinely need?
33. Does it support the payment methods your market actually uses (wallets, BNPL, local methods)?
34. Does address autofill work on mobile?
35. Are errors inline and specific, or does the form reject the whole page?
36. Is progress visible so the buyer knows how many steps remain?
37. Is there a phone number or contact route at the checkout step for high-value orders?

## Surface 5 — Trust (10 checks)
38. ★ Within 5 seconds, is there evidence a real business is behind this?
39. ★ Is there a real contact method — not only a form?
40. Is there a physical address or company detail somewhere findable?
41. Are the returns, shipping and privacy policies written, specific, and dated?
42. Is social proof verifiable, or are review counts and badges unlinked and unsourced?
43. Are "as seen in" logos linked to the actual coverage?
44. Is there any fake urgency — countdown timers or stock counts you can't substantiate? (Score 0 if yes.)
45. Does the About page describe a business, or is it stock photos and adjectives?
46. Are product photos original, or stock images a buyer can reverse-search to a competitor?
47. Is the domain, email domain, and brand name consistent across the site?

## Surface 6 — Mobile (8 checks)
48. ★ Does the product page hero load in under ~3 seconds on a mid-range phone on 4G?
49. ★ Is any critical action reachable one-handed without a zoom?
50. Are tap targets large enough to hit without precision?
51. Does the sticky header eat so much viewport that content is cramped?
52. Do popups appear before the visitor has seen anything, and can they be dismissed one-handed?
53. Is text legible without zoom at default settings?
54. Do images use correct aspect ratios, or does the layout shift as they load?
55. Does the site work in dark mode without invisible text?

## Surface 7 — Retention & Email (7 checks)
56. ★ Do you have abandoned cart, welcome, and post-purchase flows live at all?
57. ★ Does the post-purchase flow set delivery expectations precisely?
58. Is email capture offering something, or just "subscribe for updates"?
59. Do flows stop when someone buys, or do they keep selling what's already purchased?
60. Is there a winback for lapsed buyers past their natural repurchase window?
61. Do you ask for reviews after use, not after delivery?
62. Is support ticket volume being read as research, or only as a queue to clear?
