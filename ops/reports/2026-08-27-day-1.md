## What the research changed

Original pitch was "60 prompts." Two corrections, both made:

1. **Count cut to the truth (19).** Competitors sell 1,000 prompts for $19.99 and 100 for free.
   Competing on count is a losing game we'd also have to lie to enter. Every claimed number in
   every file now matches a `find` count.
2. **Reframed from list to system.** Verified merchant complaints — "sounds robotic", "can't write
   in my tone of voice", AI "invents product features that don't exist" — are context failures.
   Added two prompts nobody else covers: bulk 1k+ SKU catalogs, and the AI-tells edit pass.
3. **Removed a claim we can't support.** Merchants who A/B tested AI-generated email flows found
   they performed _worse_. The listing now says so, which costs us a selling point and buys the
   only position open in a category where a competitor advertises "4.8/5 – Rated by 5,000+" on a
   store page showing 0 ratings.
